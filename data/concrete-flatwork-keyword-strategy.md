## TL;DR — The honest call

**Do not launch a paid Google Ads campaign on "concrete flatwork" keywords.** The Utah search volume is too small to sustain spend, and "flatwork" is industry jargon that consumers don't use. Ship the page for SEO, then run a residential-intent paid campaign on **consumer-facing concrete keywords** (driveway, patio, garage floor, contractor) — but only after you pull fresh keyword data on those terms. ## 6. Search vs. Performance Max

**Launch Search-only.** Reasons:
1. PMax mixes Display, Discover, YouTube, and Search — you can't see which placement converted, and contractors typically waste 60%+ of PMax spend on low-intent Display impressions until you have 50+ conversions to train the model.
2. The site has no Merchant Center feed (this is service work, not e-commerce) — PMax's strongest mode is unavailable.
3. Search-only gives you keyword-level negatives, which is the most important spend-control lever for a niche local service.

**When to add PMax:** After the Search campaign has logged ~30 closed-loop conversions (booked appointments, not just leads) and you have a defensible asset library. Until then, every dollar in PMax buys lower-quality impressions than the same dollar in Search.

**Search campaign settings:**
- Bid strategy: **Manual CPC with Enhanced** for first 2 weeks, then switch to **Maximize Conversions** once you have 15+ conversions in 30 days. Skip Target CPA until 30+ conversions.
- Daily budget: $30–$50/day to start. Don't go higher until CPL is known.
- Audience: Layer in observation-only "Home & Garden" and "Recently Moved" affinity for reporting; do not target-narrow with them.
- Devices: bid +20% on mobile (most "near me" residential search is mobile).
- Hours: M–Sat 7am–8pm. Pause Sundays for first 30 days.

## 7. Spend on ads, or just ship for SEO?

**Honest answer: Ship for SEO first. Defer paid spend until the page proves it converts organically.**

The page already has:
- A hero CTA (call + book appointment)
- The full anxiety stack and FAQs targeting the "will it crack?" objection
- Comparison table positioning concrete vs. pavers vs. asphalt
- JSON-LD Service + FAQPage schema for rich results
- The same lead pipeline as every other BaseScape service

That's enough to capture organic and direct-traffic conversions while you watch what happens. Ship it, give it 30–60 days, and let GSC tell you which queries are actually finding the page in Utah. Those queries are your real keyword list — far more reliable than the CSVs you have today.

**The paid trigger:** If after 60 days the page is generating 3+ organic leads/month, spin up the $30–50/day Search test on driveway + garage floor ad groups. If it's not generating leads organically, the offer or page itself is the problem and ads will only amplify the leak.

The Phase 4 trade analysis flagged 17-point distance-from-threshold and freeze-thaw post-sale risk. Don't compound that risk by spending paid budget on an unproven page before you know whether it converts.
